## Who I am

I build full-stack products and AI agent systems for clients outside Pakistan — mostly in the US, UK, and Middle East. Two and a half years in, my work sits at the intersection of solid backend engineering and practical AI: things that ship, hold up under real usage, and actually save someone time.

I'm not chasing buzzwords. If an AI agent doesn't remove a manual step or a workflow doesn't get faster, I don't consider it done.

## What I actually build

**AI Agent Systems** — autonomous agents on OpenAI, Claude, and Gemini that handle real tasks: lead qualification, support triage, internal automations, MCP-based tool integrations.

**Full-Stack Web Apps** — React and Next.js on the frontend, ASP.NET Core or Node.js on the backend, built to scale past the MVP stage without a rewrite.

**SaaS & Enterprise Platforms** — clinic management systems, attendance platforms, ERP/CRM tools — the unglamorous systems businesses run on every day.
